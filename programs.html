RECOGNITIONS & ACCREDITATIONS

The UST College of Information and Computing Sciences upholds rigorous national standards in technical education, research output, and student development. Through continuous curriculum alignment, modern computing facilities, and sustained community engagement, the college has earned key distinctions from national regulatory and accrediting bodies.

CENTER OF DEVELOPMENT (COD)

The Commission on Higher Education (CHED) formally recognizes UST CICS as a Center of Development for its Computer Science, Information Systems, and Information Technology degree programs. This designation reflects the college's sustained institutional capability, strong faculty development initiatives, cutting-edge laboratory infrastructure, and research contributions across all three computing disciplines.

PAASCU LEVEL II RE-ACCREDITATION

The Philippine Accrediting Association of Schools, Colleges and Universities (PAASCU) has granted Level II Re-Accreditation status to the college's three undergraduate programs: BS Computer Science, BS Information Systems, and BS Information Technology. This credential certifies that CICS continuously meets and exceeds academic criteria in faculty credentials, instruction quality, laboratory management, student development services, and operational governance.
